**1. Core pitch**
Built LiveCaptionN: live captions + translation that float on top of ANY
Android app — not just the ones that ship their own subtitles. Runs fully
on-device (Vosk + Google ML Kit), free forever, open source. 🎧💬
[link]

**2. Privacy angle**
Your captions never leave your phone. LiveCaptionN does speech-to-text and
translation 100% on-device — no account, no cloud, no server required.
Free, open source, Android 10+.
[link]

**3. Use-case: language learning**
Watching foreign-language shows to learn a language? LiveCaptionN puts a
draggable live-translated caption overlay on top of whatever app you're
watching — YouTube, TikTok, anything. On-device, free, open source.
[link]

**4. Use-case: accessibility**
Android's built-in Live Caption doesn't translate. LiveCaptionN does —
same instant word-by-word streaming captions, plus on-device translation,
floating over any app. Free forever.
[link]
